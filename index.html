What you can do

Dual N-Back: working memory training for iPhone

A position. A sound. A match to remember. Practise the dual n-back exercise on your iPhone and follow your accuracy as the challenge changes.

Two streams, one challenge

Watch a position on the grid and listen to a letter. Compare each with what appeared N turns earlier.

Find your level

Begin with N=1, then explore higher levels and additional streams when you are ready for a different challenge.

Make progress visible

Review your session results and practise short cognitive games, including reaction time, Stroop and visual memory.

Your scores describe performance on these tasks. The app does not measure IQ or promise an increase in intelligence.

Questions

What is dual n-back training?

Dual n-back is a working-memory task: compare a position and a sound with the ones presented N turns earlier. The two streams are evaluated separately.

Can I start as a beginner?

Start with N=1 to learn the rhythm, then explore N=2 and higher levels. Session results help you understand your accuracy on the task, not your IQ.
